Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Prequel Quest helps families capture and share their stories. This page explains what this website collects, why, and what happens to it. The short version: we only collect what you choose to give us, we use it to send you Prequel Quest updates, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • The postcard sign-up: your email address.
  • Our updates form: your email address and, if you choose to add them, your name and the topics you're interested in.

That's all. There are no accounts on this site, and we never ask for payment details.

How we use it

We use your information to send you news about Prequel Quest: free story tools, community and service events, heritage trips and family history research help. We don't sell, rent or trade it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

Sign-ups are stored in Google Forms, under the Prequel Quest Google account. Only the Prequel Quest team can see them. Google stores that data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't use analytics, ad trackers or tracking pixels.

Services that help deliver this page

Like most websites, this one relies on a few outside services. When they deliver part of the page, they see basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies our typefaces.
  • Unsplash serves one of our photographs.
  • Google Forms receives your sign-up when you send it.

We don't receive or keep those technical details ourselves.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you ask us to stop sending updates or to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop getting updates, at any time. Email callmkenneth@gmail.com and we'll take care of it within 30 days.

Children

Prequel Quest is made for families, but this website isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Questions

Email callmkenneth@gmail.com. A real person reads every message.
